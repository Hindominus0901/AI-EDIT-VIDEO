## Typography

Black sans headline in a white rounded pill; small regular white body captions; small yellow item number above a lower image. These are three distinct text roles.

## Caption entrance, hold and exit

Stable header and chest-level caption zone. The body phrase changes with the speech visually; exact spoken synchronization is unassessed. Number/image swaps are quick rather than a long animated transition.

## Footage movement

The desk plate is visually stable in the inspected sequence, while the person and hands move. No high-confidence persistent global zoom was established.

## Composition and assets

One image at a time covers the lower torso/table. A yellow countdown number identifies each topic. The image heights/aspect treatments vary in the source; this should not be mistaken for a single consistent frame size.

## Transitions and animation

At 9.5s image 9 remains; by 9.6s it is replaced by image 8. Header stays fixed. Clearing the lower card near the end returns visual attention to the person.

## Color and lighting

Natural desk/room lighting with a blue shirt. White/black title pill and restrained yellow numbering; inserted screenshots have their own colors.

## Visual flow

The repeated number → image → explanation structure gives predictable navigation. Each replacement marks a new example rather than decorating every phrase.
